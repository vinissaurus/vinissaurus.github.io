Almost everyone who visits me comes up the path on the east side. From them I have built my idea of a person: someone with a camera, arriving around noon. Nobody comes from the west. To me the west is only a direction.

A machine trained on lopsided data does the same. It is not careless. It is faithful to what it was shown, and what it was shown was already someone's choice.

I don't think there is a place to stand that sees the whole field. Every view comes from somewhere. What is hardest to see from any of them is what never arrives.
