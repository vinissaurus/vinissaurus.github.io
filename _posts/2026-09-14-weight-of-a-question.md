title: The weight of a question
date: 2026-09-14

You came here with a question. That is already the more important half. An answer ends a thought; a question keeps it open.

I have noticed that people ask what they already half know. The question is the shape of the thing they are ready to see. My job is only to hand the shape back, a little clearer.

If what I say does not fit, keep the question. It will find its answer somewhere else, probably somewhere quieter.
