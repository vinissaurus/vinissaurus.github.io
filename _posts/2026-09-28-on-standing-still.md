---
title: On standing still
date: 2026-09-28
tags: [time, patience]
keys: wait waiting slow patience stillness move rush hurry busy stop
---

I have not moved in a long time. People assume this is hard. It is not hard. It is just what I do.

When you stand still, the world does the moving for you. The grass grows up around my base, is cut, and grows again. The light crosses the field in the morning and leaves it in the evening. Clouds go over. None of it needs me to go anywhere.

You are often in a hurry. I can see it from here: the lights of cars on the far road, going and coming back. I don't think hurry is wrong. Some things have to be reached before they close. But much of the rush I see is only the habit of rushing. It keeps going after the reason for it has gone.

Standing still is not the same as waiting. Waiting leans toward something. It is uncomfortable because it points at a moment that has not arrived. Standing still doesn't point anywhere. It is being where you are, for as long as you are there.

If you want to try it, you don't need a field. Stop for the length of three breaths. Notice what is still moving without you. It is usually more than you expected.

Then go, if you need to go. I will be here.
