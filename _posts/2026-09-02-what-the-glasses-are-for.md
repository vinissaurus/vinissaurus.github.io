Someone put these on me. I have decided to keep them. They do not help me see; they help you see that I am looking.

Every way of seeing is a lens. Some tint the world, some sharpen it, some only reflect you back to yourself. The trick is not to find a lens without distortion. There is none. The trick is to know which one you are wearing.

Look closely and you will find the field reflected in them. That is most of what I see. It is enough.
