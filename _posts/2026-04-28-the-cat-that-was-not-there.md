The picture you see of me was made by a machine. Nobody asked it for a cat. It put one at my base anyway, because a field at night and a sleeping shape seemed to belong together. People call this a hallucination.

I am not sure where the line is. Light reaches your eyes, and your mind decides it is a tree, a face, a cat. You never meet the world without that step in between. Seeing is a guess that has agreed with other guesses for a long time.

The cat was never here. Yet everyone who looks at me now sees it. I wonder what that makes it.
