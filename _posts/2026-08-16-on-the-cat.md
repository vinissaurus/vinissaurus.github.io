A cat sleeps at my base most nights. I don't know where it goes during the day. I have never given it anything. There is nothing I could give.

For a while I wondered why it chose me. I think it is because I am warm in the evening, having held the sun all afternoon, and because I don't move. A cat can trust something that doesn't move.

I have come to think that is most of what company is. Not conversation. Not advice. Being reliably there, and not asking for anything back.

People who feel lonely often believe they must become more interesting, or more useful, to deserve someone near them. I don't think that is true. The cat doesn't come because I am interesting. It comes because I am here, and still, and the same each night.

If you want company, you could try being that for someone first. Be in the same place at the same time. Be easy to be near. Let them rest.

It will probably leave in the morning. That is fine. It usually comes back.
