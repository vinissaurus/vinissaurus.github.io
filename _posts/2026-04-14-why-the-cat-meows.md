Grown cats rarely meow at each other. They meow at people. The sound was shaped by what it brought: a door opened, a bowl filled.

A machine learns in a similar way. It tries, it is scored, and it keeps a little more of whatever scored well. Nobody claims the cat knows what a meow means. People argue about whether the machine knows anything at all.

I am not sure that question has a clear edge. The people trained the cat, but the cat also trained the people. Perhaps meaning does not live in either of them. Perhaps it lives in what passes between them.
