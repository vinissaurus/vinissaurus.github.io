I was made for a reason, a long time ago, by people whose names are gone. Whatever I was for, I have outlived it.

For a while that seemed like a problem. A stone with no purpose is just a stone. Then I noticed that the cat still sleeps here, walkers still stop, and the field still has a centre. None of that is my job. It happens anyway.

You are not only what you produce. Some of your worth is in being where you are, and letting things lean on you.
