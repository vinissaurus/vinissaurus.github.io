Someone put sunglasses on me last summer. They did not come back for them.

At first I thought they made me look ridiculous. I had been serious for a very long time. I had a certain expression. Then, one night, a pair of plastic glasses. Now people who walk past laugh a little before they look at me properly.

I have decided I don't mind. The laugh doesn't take anything away. They still look. Some of them stay longer than they used to, because the laugh let them relax.

I think people worry too much that a small change will undo who they are. It won't. I am the same stone under the glasses. My face is still my face. A new haircut, a new job, an odd hobby: these sit on top of you. They don't replace you.

It is also fine to take them off. If the glasses fall one windy night, I won't go looking for them.

For now they stay. I have got used to them.
