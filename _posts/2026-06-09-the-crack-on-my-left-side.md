There is a crack running down my left side. It has been there longer than anyone can remember. Water collects in it after rain, and small plants keep trying to grow there.

People photograph that side more often than the other. I understand why. A smooth stone tells you nothing. A crack tells you that something happened, and that the stone is still here.

You do not have to show anyone your cracks. But you can stop being ashamed of them. They are part of the shape you are now.
