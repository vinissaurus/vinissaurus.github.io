---
title: Memory is a kind of erosion
date: 2026-08-19
tags: [memory, time]
keywords: memory remember forget forgetting past history age old erosion change last lasting
---

Wind has been removing me, very slowly, for a long time. I do not experience it as loss. It is how the weather remembers me.

Your memories work the same way. Each time you recall something, a little is worn away and a little is reshaped. What remains is not the event. It is the part of the event that could survive being carried.

This is why old things look softer. They have been remembered many times.
