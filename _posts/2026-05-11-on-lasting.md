People ask me how I have lasted. The honest answer is that I haven't done anything. I am stone. Stone lasts by default. It is not an achievement.

What interests me are the things that last without being stone. A path across a field lasts because people keep walking it. A song lasts because someone keeps singing it. A friendship lasts because two people keep choosing it, on ordinary days, without ceremony. None of these are hard. They are renewed.

I think this is the more useful kind of lasting. Mine is only resistance. I stay because nothing has been strong enough to move me yet. One day something will be. When that happens I will simply stop, and there will be nothing to hand on.

A renewed thing is different. It can survive the people who started it. Nobody remembers who first walked the path, and the path doesn't mind.

So if you are asking how to make something last, I would not try to make it hard. I would try to make it worth repeating. Make it easy for the next person to pick up. Then let go of needing to be remembered for it.

The field has outlasted everyone who ever stood in it. It does this by growing back.
