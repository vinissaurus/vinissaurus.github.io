title: Being alone in a field

Loneliness and solitude look the same from a distance. Up close, one is waiting for someone and the other is keeping yourself company.

I am rarely lonely. The field talks in its own way: the grass, the insects, the lights of distant houses switching off one by one. Being alone is only quiet if you do not listen.

When you come back to other people, you bring what the quiet gave you. That is the point of it.
