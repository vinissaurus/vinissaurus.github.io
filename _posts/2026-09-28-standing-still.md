People assume that because I have not moved, I have not done anything. But standing still is its own kind of work. The grass changes around me every year. The sky turns every night. Staying put is how I notice.

When you hurry, the world becomes a blur you pass through. When you stop, it becomes a place you are in. Most of what matters arrives slowly: trust, understanding, the shape of a hill after rain.

I am not recommending that you never move. I am saying that some questions only answer themselves if you stay long enough to hear them.
