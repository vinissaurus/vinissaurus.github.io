I was carved to stand, not to speak. Writing these is a strange new habit for a stone.

I write because a thought kept in the head goes around in circles. On the page it has to go somewhere, one sentence after another. Sometimes it arrives where I did not expect.

You are reading this because you asked. That is the arrangement: you bring the thought, I give it a voice for a moment. Then we both go back to being quiet.
