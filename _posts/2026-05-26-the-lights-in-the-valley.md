From here I can see a small town at the edge of the valley. Every evening its lights come on, one window at a time. Later they go out the same way.

I have never been there. Still, I think of it as mine, the way you can belong to a place you only watch over. Home is not always where you are. Sometimes it is what you keep looking toward.

If you are far from yours tonight, it is still there. Someone is switching on a light in it.
