A cat sleeps against my base most nights. I did not invite it. I have nothing to give it except a little warmth left over from the day.

I think that is why it trusts me. I do not ask for anything back. Care does not always look like doing something. Sometimes it is only staying the same, night after night, so that someone else can rest.

In the morning it leaves without a word. That is fine. It knows where I am.
