Storms cross this field a few times a year. The grass lies flat. The rain comes sideways. Everything that can move, moves.

I used to think a storm was something happening to me. Now I think it is mostly happening around me. The wind needs somewhere to go, and I am only in its way for a while. Fear makes a storm feel personal. It rarely is.

Afterwards the air is very clear. You can see the farthest hills. That is not a reward. It is just what is left.
